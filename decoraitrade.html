Project Overview

Decorait Trader is a premium luxury furniture and interior décor marketplace built to provide homeowners, architects, interior designers and trade professionals with an elegant online shopping experience. The platform features curated collections of luxury furniture, lighting, home décor, accessories and designer brands while supporting custom furniture requests, trade pricing and appointment booking. As the Lead UI/UX Designer, I designed the complete user experience from information architecture to high-fidelity responsive interfaces. My focus was on creating a clean, premium shopping journey that makes discovering thousands of luxury products simple, visually engaging and conversion-focused across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

Lead UI/UX Designer responsible for designing the complete luxury furniture marketplace, including product discovery, shopping flow, responsive layouts and reusable design system.
