Project Overview

IT Learning Institute is an educational platform focused on providing practical IT training and career-oriented programs for students. The website was designed to simplify course discovery, admissions, certificate verification and student engagement while maintaining a modern, responsive and professional user experience. The objective was to build a conversion-focused website that increases student trust, highlights placement success and makes important information such as courses, demo classes, resources and contact details easily accessible.

Designed a modern educational platform focused on improving course discovery, student engagement, admissions and overall learning experience.
