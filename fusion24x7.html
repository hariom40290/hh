Project Overview

Fusion24X7 is a large-scale Enterprise Business Management Platform developed to streamline HR, CRM, Payroll, Attendance, Project Management, Finance, Assets and various enterprise operations through a single unified ecosystem. I worked as the Sole UI/UX Designer for the product, owning the complete design lifecycle from understanding business requirements to crafting intuitive user experiences. In addition to designing the interfaces, I independently developed reusable React JSX components with a scalable SCSS architecture, ensuring pixel-perfect implementation and consistency across the product. The platform includes 200+ enterprise screens and 100+ business modules, supporting complex business workflows while maintaining usability, performance and a modern design language.
